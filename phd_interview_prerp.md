# COPEWATER PhD Interview: Scientific Synthesis Matrix

| Source / Paper | Architecture & Scale (The "How") | Behavior & Theory (The "Why") | Validation & Rigor (The "Proof") | **Interview "Hook"** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **de Bruijn et al. (2023)** | **Field-scale HRUs:** Dynamically sized units within a grid to represent individual farms [1, 2]. | **Heterogeneity:** Statistically representative populations of 10M+ individual agents [3, 4]. | **Landmark Milestone:** Daily bidirectional interactions between ABM and hydrology on a laptop [3]. These are based on meteorological data considering land use and crops. | "Simulated 10 million agents using dynamically sized HRUs (Hydrological Resource Unit) to bridge the farm-to-basin gap." Point to simple heuristics used (assets and income) and suggest threat and coping appraisals to better evaluate agent behavior (more realism). - Directly leads into PMT. |
| **Wens et al. (2020)** | **Cognitive Loops:** Integrated threat-coping cycles directly with soil moisture dynamics [5]. | **PMT Framework:** Decisions driven by perceived severity (Threat) and self-efficacy (Coping) [5, 6]. | **Empirical Grounding:** Calibrated using local Kenyan catchments with high efficiency [5]. Create a drought disaster memory based on prior risk exposure, damages  and harvest loss. | "Moved beyond 'rational choice' to model cognitive responses to hazard using PMT." - conclusion is that the human daptation decisions are best described assuming bounded rational adaptive behavior. |
| **Chapuis et al. (2022)** | **Empowerment Modeling:** Models used as shared vision tools to facilitate local adaptation [7]. | **Local Empowerment:** Focuses on rendering socio-hydro systems resilient via stakeholder co-design [7]. | **Participatory Validation:** Validated through iterative workshops with local actors [7]. | "Modeling as a tool for local empowerment and co-construction of adaptation measures." |
| **Ward (2026)** | **Legacy Systems:** Structural architecture of water systems designed for 20th-century conditions [8]. | **Institutional Fragmentation:** Identifies "governance mismatches" as a primary hurdle [8]. | **Policy Review:** Systematic review of ten key policy challenges in water planning [8, 9]. | "Identified 'institutional fragmentation' as the primary barrier to adaptive water governance." |
| **Perevochtchikova (2025)** | **Cross-Scale Analysis:** SES trajectories analyzed across various temporal and spatial scales [10]. | **SES Trajectories (SEST):** Constant non-linear co-evolution of human and ecological components [10, 11]. | **Systematic Review:** Synthesis of 143 articles to define SES evolution patterns [11, 12]. | "Defined SES Trajectories to understand the non-linear co-evolution of water-human systems." |
| **Xiong et al. (2026)** | **Micro-Macro Links:** Analyzes how individual behaviors aggregate into system-level behaviors [13]. | **Policy Laboratory:** Evaluates ABMs as environments for testing future management scenarios [13]. | **Pattern-Oriented Validation:** advocates for reproducing independent real-world patterns [14]. | "Proposed POV to fix the 'validation deficit' by matching real-world patterns, not just means." |
| **Taillandier (2019)** | **Multi-level Modeling:** Building and composing complex spatial models within GAMA [15, 16]. | **GAMA Standard:** The definitive platform for integrating social and natural dynamics [16, 17]. | **Built-in Experimentation:** Tools for rigorous sensitivity analysis and spatial complexity [16]. | "The foundational paper for GAMA, enabling multi-level spatial complexity and multi-agent logic." |
| **COPEWATER Offer (S8)** | **Staged Coupling:** Moving from *Loose Coupling* to a *Dynamic Machine Learning Proxy* [18]. | **Crisis Memory:** Human choices shaped by the 2023–24 water crisis and perceived risk [18]. | **Retro/Prospective:** Replaying past crises to analyze mechanisms and explore future paths [19]. | "Loose coupling is the first step; the ML proxy restores the dynamic feedback loop." |
